**Urban Place Perception in Seoul  A District-Level Sentiment Analysis of Instagram Location-Based Social Media**
*Hanyang University, Department of Smart City Engineering*

Collected and analyzed Instagram data from across Seoul to study the relationship between how people perceive places in each district and the district's local economic characteristics. Used AI tools for data collection (Apify), Python, sentiment analysis (XLM-RoBERTa), correlation analysis, and spatial visualization.
